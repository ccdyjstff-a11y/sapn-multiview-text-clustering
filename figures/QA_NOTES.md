# Executed plotting QA

Three portable redraws were executed with Python 3.10.22, NumPy 2.2.6 and Matplotlib 3.10.9. No model fitting or category-label scoring was involved. The numerical and export checks below are separate from journal style certification.

- Figure 13: 1,503 analytical response rows (501 frequencies and three strengths) match the retained source values exactly. The conditional-risk identities were checked at 4,509 values; recorded numerical residuals are in `generated/NUMERICAL_CHECKS.json`. This is an analytical illustration, without empirical error bars.
- Figure 21: all 400 paired ACC values and 40 groups of ten seeds were retained. Means, sample SD, signs and the fixed full/zoom scales match the source tables. No seed was removed from summaries. `qa/fig21/data_integrity.json` records these checks. Switching from the externally installed alignment helper to the standalone study helper did not change preview bytes. The resulting PNG is also byte-identical to the frozen paper preview.
- Figure 22: all 40 paired ACC values and eight groups of five seeds were retained. Mean and sample SD match the sealed reporting CSVs to the explicit 1e-12 check. Its PNG is byte-identical to the frozen paper preview. AG News consensus contrasts include negative values and remain shown. `generated/fig22_plot_checks.json` binds the two input CSVs.

The final axes geometry gate passes for all three figures. A visual inspection of each full PNG found readable labels, visible markers/error bars and no cropped content. `qa/EXPORT_BOUNDS.json` records one page per PDF, zero words outside the page, and exact word-text and bounding-box equality with the corresponding frozen manuscript figures. Exported PDF text was inspected at a 5 pt floor. The source and text-audit JSONs are retained.

The generic source validator passes figures 13 and 21 with nonblocking warnings (for example, a PNG preview without a TIFF submission raster). Figure 22 deliberately preserves its frozen source style and has three generic source-style failures: DejaVu Sans is absent from that tool's publication-font allowlist, SVG uses glyph paths, and its preview uses 260 dpi. These do not change the plotted values or PDF text, and are not reported as a passed journal-style gate. Its PAA-facing delivery is the vector PDF, with embedded TrueType text at 9 pt or larger; its SVG is vector geometry with outlined glyphs. The PNG is an inspection preview, not a claimed submission raster. No source-style policy is silently changed to manufacture a clean audit.

PyMuPDF is unavailable in this environment. No new PyMuPDF text/stroke collision pass is claimed. Poppler text bounds, unchanged frozen text geometry, numerical checks and visual inspection were used instead. The study-authored alignment helper checks axes rectangles and available panel-label anchors; it does not inspect every text-to-line or data-marker collision.

Original manuscript assets and original plotting scripts were not edited. `SOURCE_ORIGINS.json` binds the retained originals and portable derivatives; `GENERATED_ARTIFACTS.json` binds the delivered own-figure exports. PDF serialization metadata may differ on another redraw even when the visible output and numerical data agree.
