For each corpus and seed, pool-excluded relation BER uses a fixed uniform sample without replacement of unordered pairs from evaluation objects. The pair count is $\min\{10000,\binom{n_{\rm eval}}{2}\}$ and the sampling seed is the run seed plus 51000. The same pairs are used across budgets and methods. The sampler retains first occurrences from an independent uniform pair stream. BER averages the must-link and cannot-link error rates; if either sign is absent, BER is undefined (stored as None), rather than zero. All 50 saved evaluation-pair sets contain both signs. Counts and source hashes are provided in the accompanying table. Pairs can share endpoints and are not independent experimental replicates; uncertainty summaries remain at the run level.
