# Evidence-to-claim map

All paths below are direct files in this local release. They become publicly or reviewer accessible only after the owner establishes an access route. The original archive, SHA256 `3e0e1c75574678d0c0a2026afe98a7b9ab58bc283eac453544e1d3673b044179`, remains a separate immutable historical attachment. `provenance/ARCHIVE_ORIGINS.json` binds extracted members to that archive. This directory does not claim to contain every intermediate state in the historical research tree.

The separately sealed field-corpus extension is in `evidence/allocation_extension/`: protocol, freeze/input/global seals, all ten terminals and paid-query streams, 50 logical finals mapped to 39 compact predictions, 30 selections, all fold/final diagnostics, reporting CSVs and the independent post-seal 250-value rescoring receipt. All 219 unique validation/final fits were eligible. These are 20NG and AG News representations already used in the study, not never-seen independent datasets; unfavorable contrasts remain visible. The export manifest and omission ledger distinguish included evidence from omitted raw inputs and optimizer states.

All 14 figure assets (13 preceding figures plus the new extension) have explicit data and plotting-source pointers in [the figure map](figures/README.md). Three portable redraws were executed: analytical reference risk, main allocation and field allocation. The remaining original research generators are available for inspection with historical dependency limits.
